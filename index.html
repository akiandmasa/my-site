About me

Hi, I'm Jake.

A quiet person with a big drive to help people in need.

My story

I'm quiet by nature, but when it comes to keeping people safe online, I'm all in.

I'm the CEO of The Trusted Community (TTC), an online safety and trust community that protects Discord users.

I'm also learning to code with the goal of becoming a cybersecurity professional, so I can protect people on a much bigger scale.

Away from the screen I'm usually thinking about space, practicing languages, watching anime or listening to music. And yes, I really do hate sleeping.

I love anime and music so much, but anime hits different.
